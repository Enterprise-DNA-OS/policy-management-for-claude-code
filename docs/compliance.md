# Policy evidence checks

Checked 17 October 2026. These checks support human review of records. They do not establish that a policy is lawful, that consultation was meaningful, or that a person understood a document.

| Rule | Check | Source and limit |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-HSWA-01 | Safety revisions need a consultation evidence reference before becoming effective. Drafts without one appear in the review list. | WorkSafe says businesses must involve workers in workplace health and safety. The evidence field supports that process; it does not assess its quality. [Worker engagement and participation](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/managing-health-and-safety/businesses/worker-engagement-and-participation/) |
| NZ-IPP9 | People with missing or elapsed retention-review dates appear for review. | Privacy Act principle 9 limits retention to the lawful purpose. Dates are chosen by the business after considering applicable record requirements; the software has no universal destruction period and deletes nothing. [Privacy Commissioner, principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) |
| POL-01 | Review date is before today. Upcoming review desk looks 30 days ahead. | Internal policy cadence, not a statutory deadline. |
| POL-02 | Owner is inactive. | Internal ownership control. |
| POL-03 | Open exception expiry is before today. | Internal exception control. |

Independent approval, immutable approved text, one effective version and version-specific acknowledgements are internal governance controls. They are not a vendor certification or a legal signature system. Evidence references must point to retained material in the business's controlled archive. Operators verify that source material and record the actor. Local names are not authentication.

A safety change missing consultation evidence cannot be repaired after approval by editing its text or evidence reference. Create a new revision and obtain a fresh decision. Rejected revisions need a new revision. Acknowledgements can only be recorded for the current effective version and within its effective period. Historic evidence from an old system is retained separately during migration; it is not silently converted into current acknowledgements.

Configure local law and retention schedules before operational use outside New Zealand. An empty findings list means no configured record gap was found; it is not compliance certification.
